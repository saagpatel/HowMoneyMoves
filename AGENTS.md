# Portfolio Context

## What This Project Is

HowMoneyMoves: HowMoneyMoves walks you through the actual infrastructure of US banking: how a paycheck becomes an ACH file, how it clears through the Federal Reserve's settlement network, how SWIFT wires reach foreign correspondent banks, and how fractional reserve multiplies deposits. Each section is a narrated Framer Motion animation with step-by-step controls, sourced from NACHA, the Fed, and the APA — plus error-case callouts showing exactly what breaks and why.
